## Becoming a maintainer

Maintainer access is earned through sustained contributions that demonstrate sound judgment,
respect for users and upstream maintainers, reliable review, and care for the project's safety
invariants. Generated volume is not a qualification.

Nominations are discussed by existing maintainers and recorded in a governance pull request.

## Emeritus status

Maintainers who step away may be listed as emeritus. Access should be removed when it is no longer
needed, without diminishing prior contributions.
